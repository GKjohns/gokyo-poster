# New Analogy Concepts (Round 2)

Round 2 replacement concept for Uchi Mata only. Round 1's barrier arm rendered between the
legs at the crotch in 5/5 side views, so the concept is swapped for one where the lifting
element naturally sits under ONE thigh and the top of the body is already leaning forward.

Same house rules as `concepts.md`: mechanically faithful, one drawable frozen frame,
explainable to a child, whimsical enough to clear gpt-image-2 safety, no broom or sweeping
tool, no barrier arm, turnstile or gate, and no reuse of a scene already on the poster.

---

## 12. Uchi Mata — *firm replacement* (`data/group-2.json`)

**Mechanic in one line:** tori's leg rises powerfully between uke's thighs and lifts ONE
inner thigh skyward while the hands pull the upper body forward and around, so uke rotates
over the rising leg with the legs high behind.

**Candidates**

| # | Concept | Why it maps (one line) |
|---|---|---|
| a | **Stepping over the sleeping dog** who stands up hindquarters-first under your trailing thigh | The rising rump lifts one inner thigh from below while your forward-leaning step supplies the pull: one leg to the sky, head over the dog. |
| b | **Dolphin surfaces under your leg** while you wade forward through the shallows | A broad back rising under one thigh from behind and below, with the wader already leaning forward, so the top goes over and the leg goes up. |
| c | **Sprinter clips a springy sapling** with the trailing inner thigh and it whips upward | The spring lifts one thigh while full-sprint lean pitches the body forward. Cut: hane-goshi's candidate already owns the sapling. |

**Ranking.** (a) first: it is a scene everyone with a big dog has lived, the dog's back is a
broad seat rather than a bar so the lift can not read as a poke at the crotch, dogs really do
stand up rump-first, and the mid-step lean forward is a natural hand-pull. (b) second: same
lift-from-below geometry, water hides the contact point even further, but it is a fantasy
scene rather than a lived one. (c) dropped for the sapling overlap.

### 12a. `dog-stands-up` — TOP PICK

- **analogy_name:** `Stepping over the dog who stands up`
- **analogy:** `You are stepping over the big dog asleep across the hallway, front foot already down on the far side, back leg still passing over him. He picks that moment to stand up, back end first. His rump rises under your inner thigh, that leg goes straight up toward the ceiling, and since you were already leaning into the step you go head-first over his ears.`
- **why_it_maps:** `The dog's rising back is tori's sweeping leg, lifting from the inside of one thigh, and your forward lean into the step is the hand-pull. Lift below, lean above, one leg skyward and the head goes over: that is uchi mata.`
- **image_prompt:**

low near the floor, so its back slopes steeply DOWN from tail to nose; a laughing child

### 12b. `dolphin-surfaces` — SECOND

- **analogy_name:** `Dolphin surfaces under your leg`
- **analogy:** `You are wading forward through a warm lagoon, leaning into the water, when a dolphin surfaces right under your back leg. Its back lifts your inner thigh, that leg swings up out of the water toward the sky, and since you were already leaning forward you dive clean over its head, snorkel and all.`
- **why_it_maps:** `The dolphin's back rising from below and behind is tori's sweeping leg lifting one inner thigh; your forward lean into the wade is the hand-pull. Lifted at the thigh and already committed forward at the top, you rotate over the rising back.`
