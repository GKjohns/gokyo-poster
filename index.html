Gokyo Analogies

All 40 throws of the traditional Gokyo no Waza, each paired with the everyday scene that carries its mechanics. Review pass — images and styling come later.

Dai Ikkyo 第一教

1.1

Deashi Harai 出足払

Advancing Foot Sweep

Just as uke's advancing foot is about to take weight, tori sweeps it sideways with the sole of his foot while his hands pull down and across. The support disappears at the exact moment it's needed, and uke's body keeps traveling with nothing under it.

Stepping onto a runaway skateboard

You stride confidently forward and your front foot lands on a skateboard you didn't see. The instant your weight commits, the board scoots your foot out ahead of you, and the rest of you drops flat where the foot used to be.

The advancing foot is carried away sideways at the precise moment of weight transfer, exactly like the timed sweep of deashi harai.

image prompt

A person mid-stride on a sidewalk whose front foot has just landed on a skateboard that is shooting out ahead of them; the foot is being carried away on the board while their body, arms windmilling, hangs in the air beginning to fall flat; the gap between the sliding foot and the falling body is the focus of the composition.

1.2

Hiza Guruma 膝車

Knee Wheel

Tori places the sole of his foot against the side of uke's knee, fixing it in place, while his hands pull uke's upper body around and over. The stopped knee becomes the axle and uke's whole body wheels over it.

Over the handlebars

A kid on a bike rolls into a low curb. The front wheel stops dead, but the rider doesn't: the bike pivots over the jammed wheel and the kid sails clean over the handlebars.

A low fixed block (the knee, the stopped wheel) becomes a pivot while momentum above it keeps going, so the body rotates over the block like a wheel around its axle.

image prompt

A child on a bicycle whose front wheel has just slammed into a low concrete curb; the bike is tipping forward, rear wheel lifting off the ground, and the helmeted rider is mid-air arcing over the handlebars, body rotating around the stopped front wheel; side view showing the curb, the pivoting bike, and the flying rider in one arc.

1.3

Sasae Tsurikomi Ashi 支釣込足

Propping Drawing Ankle

Tori props the front of uke's ankle with his foot, stopping the lower body cold, while his hands lift and pull the upper body forward. Feet stop, torso keeps going, and uke flips over the propped ankle.

The classic classroom trip

A kid hurries down the aisle between desks and another kid slides a sneaker out right in front of their ankle. The foot stops mid-stride; the rest of them doesn't. They pitch straight over the outstretched shoe, books sailing.

The outstretched foot propping the ankle is exactly tori's blocking foot: the base is arrested at floor level while momentum carries the upper body up and over it.

image prompt

A school classroom aisle where one seated kid has slid a sneakered foot out in front of a walking kid's ankle; the walker's foot is stopped dead against the shoe while their body pitches forward into a flat mid-air flip, books and papers flying from their arms; side view at desk height showing the propped ankle as the pivot.

1.4

Uki Goshi 浮腰

Floating Hip

Tori slips his hip in front of uke's and, with an arm around the waist, twists. Uke doesn't get lifted so much as whirled around the hip, which acts as a post his body wraps around on the way to the floor.

Slung by the revolving door

You push into a revolving door in a hurry and lean into the bar. The door takes your push, spins on its axis, and whips you around with it — slinging you sideways out of the doorway before you can get a foot under you.

The door's central axis at hip height converts your straight-ahead push into a fast rotation around it, just as tori's inserted hip turns uke's forward pressure into a wrapping spin to the floor.

image prompt

A glass revolving door mid-spin with a person being whipped around inside it, hips pressed against the push bar, feet swinging off the ground as the rotation slings them sideways out of the door's opening; view from just outside the doorway catching the body mid-whirl around the central axis.

1.5

Osoto Gari 大外刈

Major Outer Reap

Tori rocks uke's weight back onto one heel, then swings his own leg through like a scythe and reaps that supporting leg out from behind. With the weight already behind the base and the base suddenly gone, uke falls straight back.

Chair legs swept mid-lean

A kid tips his chair back onto its two rear legs, balancing at the edge. At that exact moment a broom sweeps the rear legs forward out from under him, and chair and kid crash flat onto their backs together.

Weight is first committed backward over a single narrow support, then that support is swept away in the same direction, dropping everything straight back.

image prompt

A kid leaning far back in a wooden chair balanced on its two rear legs, eyes wide, while a broom sweeps those rear chair legs forward out from underneath; chair and kid are caught at the beginning of a straight backward fall, feet and chair legs kicking up toward the ceiling.

1.6

O Goshi 大腰

Major Hip Throw

Tori drops his hips below uke's center, wraps an arm around the waist, lifts uke onto his hips, and tips forward. Uke's body rolls over the hip like a load over an edge and lands in front.

Dumped from a wheelbarrow

A gardener wheels a friend along in a wheelbarrow, then lifts the handles high. The passenger pivots over the front lip and rolls out onto the grass in a heap while the barrow tips over its front wheel.

Lifting from behind rotates the whole load over a low fixed edge at hip height, exactly how tori's raised hips become the tipping point uke rolls over.

image prompt

A gardener tipping a wheelbarrow forward by lifting its handles high; a grinning passenger inside is rolling out over the front lip of the barrow, upside down mid-tumble toward a soft lawn, the wheelbarrow pivoted steeply over its single front wheel.

1.7

Ouchi Gari 大内刈

Major Inner Reap

Tori steps in, hooks his leg around the inside of uke's leg, and reaps it out backward in the direction of uke's toes while driving the chest straight back. One support scooped from between the legs, uke sits down hard onto his back.

Hooked by the giant crook

A performer stands mid-bow when a giant shepherd's crook slides between their ankles, hooks one leg, and yanks it forward while a stagehand's palm nudges their chest. The leg shoots out and they plop straight down onto their back.

A hook pulls one support out from the inside while a push sends the body backward over the space where the leg used to be, the same two opposing forces as ouchi gari.

image prompt

A person on a stage with one leg being hooked and pulled forward at the ankle by a giant shepherd's crook reaching between their feet, while a hand pushes flat against their chest; they are tipping straight backward, arms flailing, one leg dragged up and forward by the hook.

1.8

Seoi Nage 背負投

Shoulder Throw

Tori turns his back into uke, pulls him tight so uke's weight loads onto his back, then bends and pitches him over the shoulder. Uke's own forward pressure gets redirected up, over, and down in front.

Piggyback ride gone wrong

A kid rides piggyback, arms wrapped over the carrier's shoulders. Then the carrier suddenly bends sharply forward — and the passenger keeps going, sailing over the shoulders and flipping onto the grass in front.

A load held tight against the back is rotated over the shoulder fulcrum by the sudden forward bend, exactly how uke, pulled onto tori's back, travels over the shoulder in seoi nage.

image prompt

A backyard scene where a piggyback carrier has bent sharply forward at the waist and the kid who was riding is flying over their shoulders — mid-air, upside down, about to land on the grass in front; side view emphasizing the arc from the carrier's back, over the shoulder, to the ground ahead.

Dai Nikyo 第二教

2.1

Kosoto Gari 小外刈

Minor Outer Reap

Tori clips uke's heel from the outside just as uke commits weight to it, pushing uke's upper body backward at the same moment. The support foot shoots forward and uke sits down hard onto their back.

Slipping on the banana peel

The cartoon classic: your heel lands on a banana peel and shoots forward ahead of you. Your foot goes where it was headed — just far too fast — and the rest of you drops straight back onto the ground.

The peel carries the weight-bearing heel forward out from under you exactly as the reap does, leaving nothing under your center and dropping you flat on your back.

image prompt

A person mid-slip on a sidewalk, one heel skating forward on a yellow banana peel so that leg shoots out ahead while the body tips straight backward, arms windmilling, groceries flying from a dropped bag; low side-on view so the escaping foot and the backward-falling body read in one line.

2.2

Kouchi Gari 小内刈

Minor Inner Reap

As uke steps forward, tori hooks the advancing heel from the inside and slides it further along its own path while pushing uke straight back. The foot keeps traveling, the hips never arrive, and uke sits down between their own feet.

The just-mopped floor slip

You stride across a gleaming just-mopped supermarket aisle past the yellow warning sign. Your front foot hits the wet tile and keeps on going, sliding out far ahead of you while your body sits straight down behind it.

The slip extends the stepping foot along the exact direction it was already traveling — the same 'help your step keep going' action as the inner reap — leaving the hips unsupported and falling backward.

image prompt

A shopper in a supermarket aisle mid-slip on a shiny wet floor, front foot skating far forward ahead of the body with a spray of water, rear leg buckling, body dropping into a seated backward fall, a yellow wet-floor caution cone standing nearby, viewed from the side.

2.3

Koshi Guruma 腰車

Hip Wheel

Tori wraps an arm around uke's head and neck, plants the hips deep across uke's waist, and pulls the head around. Uke's whole body wheels around the hip like a rim around an axle and lands flat on the far side.

Flipping over the playground bar

A kid folds over a low playground high-bar at the waist, and a friend gives the shoulders a pull. The bar stays put while the whole body wheels around it in one smooth circle, feet chasing head all the way over.

The fixed bar at the waist is the hip serving as an axle, and pulling the head/shoulders around it is exactly the wheel-spoke rotation that carries uke over.

image prompt

A child rotating fully around a waist-high horizontal playground bar, body captured mid-revolution with head leading downward on the far side and legs swinging up over the top, hands of a second child guiding the shoulders around, side view composed so the bar reads as the fixed axle at the center of a circular motion path.

2.4

Tsurikomi Goshi 釣込腰

Lifting-Pulling Hip

Tori's high lapel grip lifts and pulls uke up and forward — like hoisting on a line — while tori drops the hips low across uke's thighs. Drawn up onto that low fulcrum, uke pours over it head-first.

Bucket tipped over the well rim

You haul a heavy bucket up out of a well, rope hand over hand, drawing it up and toward you. The moment it meets the low stone rim it tips — pivoting on the edge and pouring out over the outside.

The rope's lift-and-draw is the tsurikomi lifting pull on the lapel, and the low stone rim is tori's dropped hip: a load drawn up onto a low edge pours straight over it.

image prompt

A person at a stone well hauling a rope hand over hand, a full wooden bucket drawn up level with the low stone rim and caught at the instant of tipping over it, water pouring down the outside of the well; side view showing the taut rope pulling up and inward and the bucket pivoting on the rim's edge.

2.5

Okuri Ashi Harai 送足払

Sliding Foot Sweep

As uke moves sideways and their feet come together, tori sweeps both feet along in the direction they were already traveling. Both legs sail away together, the body floats flat for an instant, then drops.

Wave sweeps your feet seaward

You stand ankle-deep in the surf as a strong wave rushes sideways along the beach. It scoops both feet and carries them along together, and for one weightless moment you're lying flat on the air before you splash down.

The wave takes both feet at once in the direction the body was already drifting — the same 'send the feet on ahead' action, floating the body horizontal before it falls.

image prompt

A person ankle-deep at the edge of the ocean caught at the instant a rushing sideways wave sweeps both of their feet together off the sand, legs carried sideways by the white water, body momentarily horizontal in the air above the foam, beach and shoreline receding behind, low side-on viewpoint emphasizing the swept feet and floating body.

2.6

Tai Otoshi 体落

Body Drop

Tori blocks uke's shin with an outstretched leg — a low bar across the ankle — and hurls uke's upper body forward and down past it. The feet stop dead at the block while the body keeps going, so uke pitches straight over.

Dog bolts at the low bench

You're walking a big dog past a knee-high park bench when it bolts after a squirrel. The leash snaps taut and hauls you forward and down just as your shins catch the bench edge — feet stopped, everything else launched.

The lunging leash is tori's two-handed hurl on sleeve and lapel, and the bench edge is the outstretched leg: a hard forward-down pull above a shin-height block pitches the body straight over.

image prompt

A person walking a large happy dog past a knee-high park bench at the exact moment the dog bolts after a squirrel: the leash is stretched taut and hauling them forward and down, both shins caught against the bench edge, body pitching comically headlong over it with arms outstretched, the squirrel dashing away at the edge of the frame; side view showing the taut leash line and the bench as the low pivot.

2.7

Harai Goshi 払腰

Sweeping Hip

Tori draws uke's chest forward onto the hip, then sweeps a leg back and up along uke's thighs. The legs are swept skyward while the upper body is already committed over the pivot, so uke rotates over and lands beyond it.

Broom sweeps you over the wall

You're leaning over a low garden wall reaching for something on the far side when a giant push-broom sweeps up under your thighs from behind. Your legs fly up, the wall holds your middle, and over you go.

The wall is tori's hip supporting the committed upper body while the broom is the sweeping leg lifting the thighs — legs up plus a fixed pivot at the waist equals a full rotation over.

image prompt

A person tipped over a low garden wall, upper body hanging down on the far side and both legs swept high into the air by a giant push-broom angled up under their thighs from behind, the wall pressing at their waist as the pivot point, side view composing wall, body, and broom as one rotating arc.

2.8

Uchi Mata 内股

Inner Thigh Throw

Tori's leg rises powerfully between uke's thighs, lifting one inner thigh skyward while the hands pull uke's upper body forward and around. Lifted at the middle and pulled at the top, uke rotates over the rising leg.

Flipped by the rising barrier arm

You lean out over a parking-lot barrier gate just as it swings up. The arm rises hard under your inner thigh, one leg kicks toward the sky, and with your head already out over the far side you rotate clean over the top.

The rising arm lifting under the inner thigh is tori's sweeping leg, and your forward-committed upper body is the hand-pull — lift below, rotation above, body flipped over the moving bar.

image prompt

A person being flipped over a red-and-white striped parking barrier arm caught mid-rise, the arm lifting under one inner thigh so that leg points skyward, their upper body pitched forward and down over the far side of the gate, arms reaching toward the pavement, side view freezing the exact top of the rotation.

Dai Sankyo 第三教

3.1

Kosoto Gake 小外掛

Minor Outer Hook

Tori hooks a foot behind uke's heel from the outside and pins it to the mat, then drives their weight straight into uke's chest. With the foot anchored and unable to step back, uke topples backward over their own trapped leg.

Boot stuck in the mud

One boot is sunk to the ankle in deep mud when a friend gives you a playful shove on the chest. The rescue step backward never comes — the boot won't budge — so you tip straight over it like a fence post, flat onto your back.

The mud is tori's hooking foot pinning the heel to the ground: with the support anchored, the chest-high push has nothing to do but rotate you straight over backward.

image prompt

A muddy field where one person's boot is sunk deep in the mud, leg anchored, while a friend's outstretched hand has just shoved their chest; the stuck person is tipping straight backward like a felled post, the anchored leg still upright in the mud, arms flailing. Side view emphasizing the trapped boot as the fixed point of the fall.

3.2

Tsuri Goshi 釣腰

Lifting Hip

Tori reaches over or under uke's arm and grabs the belt like a handle, hauls uke up onto the hip with that grip — 'tsuri' means fishing — and rotates them over the hip to the mat.

Hauling the big fish over the rail

A fisherman has hooked a huge fish and heaves it up out of the water on a taut line. At the top of the pull the fish swings up and over the boat's rail, pivoting on the gunwale and flopping down onto the deck on the other side.

The fishing line is the belt grip (a single handle doing all the lifting) and the boat rail is tori's hip: hoist the load up on the handle, let it pivot over the rail, and it lands on the far side.

image prompt

A small fishing boat on calm water: a fisherman leans back hauling a taut fishing line with both hands, and an enormous fish is caught mid-air at the top of the arc, swinging up and over the boat's side rail, about to flop onto the deck. The fish's body curves over the rail like a load pivoting on a fulcrum. Side view showing the line, the rail contact point, and the fish's arc.

3.3

Yoko Otoshi 横落

Side Drop

A sacrifice throw: tori deliberately falls onto their own side, sliding an extended leg against the outside of uke's feet. Tori's falling body weight, transmitted through the grips, drags uke sideways over their own blocked feet.

Dragged into the pool

Two friends stand holding wrists at the edge of a pool. One suddenly lets themselves fall in — and the raised pool edge catches the other friend's feet while the falling friend's whole weight yanks them sideways, so they pitch helplessly over the coping and into the water too.

The falling friend is tori's sacrificed body weight doing the pulling, and the raised pool edge is tori's extended leg blocking uke's feet: anchored feet plus a great weight dropping beside you equals a sideways crash.

image prompt

A backyard swimming pool with a raised stone edge: one person is mid-fall into the water, back-first with a splash beginning, still gripping the wrist of a second person on the deck. The second person's feet are caught against the raised pool edge and their body is tipping sideways over it, off balance, about to be pulled in. Side-on composition showing the edge blocking the feet and the falling body's weight dragging through the joined arms.

3.4

Ashi Guruma 足車

Leg Wheel

Tori turns in and holds an extended leg as a rigid horizontal bar across the front of uke's knees, then pulls uke's upper body forward and around. Uke's legs stop at the bar while their body keeps traveling, so they wheel around the leg like a wheel around an axle.

Flipping over the low chain fence

A kid sprints across a park and doesn't see the knee-high chain slung between two posts. The chain stops their shins cold, their upper body keeps going, and they somersault clean over it — feet flying up over their head.

The taut chain is tori's extended leg — a fixed bar at knee height — and the runner's own momentum is tori's pull: body rotates over the bar because the legs are stopped and the torso isn't.

image prompt

A park path with two short posts and a taut chain strung between them at knee height: a running child has just hit the chain with their shins and is caught mid-somersault directly above it, body inverted, legs swinging up and over their head, sneakers in the air. Side view with the chain clearly the pivot point of the flip.

3.5

Hane Goshi 跳腰

Spring Hip

Tori tucks in close with one bent, springy leg pressed across uke's thighs and the hip loaded under uke. Then the leg snaps straight with a hop — 'hane' means spring — bouncing uke up off the coiled leg and over.

Launched off the seesaw

A kid sits on the low end of a seesaw when a big kid leaps onto the high end. The plank springs up beneath them and they're launched — bounced up off the board and forward through the air in a somersault.

The plank snapping upward under the kid's body is tori's coiled leg springing straight: a loaded spring beneath the hips that suddenly releases and bounces the whole body up and over.

image prompt

A playground seesaw at the instant of launch: a large kid has just slammed down on one end, the plank is kicked steeply upward, and a small kid is flying off the rising end — airborne above the board, tipping into a forward somersault, sneakers over head. Side view showing the plank's spring and the arc of flight.

3.6

Harai Tsurikomi Ashi 払釣込足

Sweeping Drawing Ankle

Tori lifts and draws uke upward-forward with both grips — holding uke's weight almost suspended — while sweeping uke's advancing foot sideways before it can land and take weight. The feet fly out from under a body that's being held up from above.

Broom sweeps the marionette's feet

A marionette dangles from its strings, feet just brushing the stage. A broom whisks across the floor and sweeps its little wooden feet sideways — the puppet's legs sail out from under it while its body, still hanging from the strings, swings and twists in the air.

The strings are tori's lifting 'tsurikomi' pull (the support comes from above, so the feet are light), and the broom is the sweeping leg: with weight held up top, one sideways brush takes the feet completely away.

image prompt

A puppet theater stage: a wooden marionette suspended from visible strings held by a hand above, its feet swept sideways off the floor by a household broom pushed by another hand entering from the side. The puppet's legs trail horizontally in the direction of the broom stroke while its torso hangs and tilts from the taut strings. Composition shows strings taut above and both feet airborne.

3.7

Tomoe Nage 巴投

Circle Throw

As uke pushes forward, tori drops straight onto their own back, plants a foot on uke's belly, and uses uke's charging momentum plus the extending leg to sling them overhead in a full circle — 'tomoe' is a swirling comma shape.

The airplane game gone flying

A parent lies back on the grass playing airplane: foot on the kid's tummy, hands in hands. But this time the leg extends with a whoosh and the kid actually launches — soaring upside down clear over the parent's head, a full loop in the air.

It is the same machine: the parent's planted foot is tori's launching leg, the held hands are the grips steering the circle, and the kid's forward lean is uke's momentum being redirected overhead.

image prompt

A sunny backyard lawn: a parent lies flat on their back with one leg extended straight up, foot planted on a child's belly, hands gripping the child's hands. The child is airborne and inverted, mid-arc directly overhead, hair and shirt tumbling, following a circular flight path over the parent's head. Side view capturing the full circle of the trajectory.

3.8

Kata Guruma 肩車

Shoulder Wheel

Tori drops low, pulls uke's arm so their body folds across tori's shoulders — draped like a load in a fireman's carry — then stands and wheels uke across the shoulders, rolling them off the far side to the mat.

Farmer flips the grain sack

A farmer squats, hoists a huge sack of grain across the shoulders, and carries it balanced behind the neck. At the wagon, one heave — and the sack rolls end over end across the shoulders and tumbles off the far side.

The shoulders are the axle in both: a long load draped across them is lifted from one side and wheeled over to drop off the other, which is exactly what 'shoulder wheel' means.

image prompt

A farmyard beside a wooden wagon: a farmer bent at the knees with an enormous full grain sack draped across the back of their shoulders, caught at the moment of heaving it over — the sack tipping past the far shoulder, rolling end over end, about to tumble off and down. Side view showing the shoulders as the pivot point of the sack's rotation.

Dai Yonkyo 第四教

4.1

Sumi Gaeshi 隅返

Corner Reversal

Tori drops onto their back beneath uke, hooks an instep into uke's inner thigh, and uses the backward roll to loft uke's hips up and over, flipping them past the corner of their own balance.

The airplane game gone too far

A parent lies on their back playing 'airplane,' feet planted in the kid's hips, and rocks backward with just a little too much enthusiasm. The kid sails clean over the parent's head, arms out, headed for the couch cushions behind.

The parent's rolling body and lifting foot are exactly tori's backward sacrifice and thigh hook — the passenger's own forward lean supplies the flip.

image prompt

A parent lying flat on their back on a living room rug, knees bent, both socked feet planted against the hips of a small child who is airborne directly above and beyond the parent's head, mid-flip, arms spread wide, sailing toward a pile of couch cushions. Low side-on composition showing the arc of the child's flight path over the parent's head.

4.2

Tani Otoshi 谷落

Valley Drop

Tori slides a straight leg behind both of uke's heels and drops their whole body backward alongside, so uke topples over the leg like falling into a ditch that opened up behind them.

The crouching-friend playground prank

One kid quietly kneels on all fours right behind someone's knees while a second kid gives a gentle push from the front. The victim tips straight backward over the crouched kid, arms windmilling, into the soft grass.

The crouched body is tori's extended leg — a solid bar behind the knees — and the push is the backward kuzushi that leaves uke nowhere to step.

image prompt

A grassy schoolyard scene: one child kneeling on all fours directly behind a standing child's legs, while the standing child tips straight backward over the kneeler's back, arms windmilling, sneakers lifting off the ground. A third child stands in front with hands still extended from a light push. Side-on composition catching the exact moment of the backward topple.

4.3

Hane Makikomi 跳巻込

Springing Wraparound

Tori springs a bent leg up into uke's thighs, wraps an arm over uke's arm and back, and hurls their own rotating body toward the mat — uke is wound around tori like fabric around an axle and slammed down with them.

Dough wrapped around a falling rolling pin

A rolling pin spins off the edge of the kitchen counter with a sheet of dough stuck fast to it. As the pin tumbles, the dough wraps tighter around the barrel and rides it all the way down, slapping flat onto the floor still wound around the pin.

Tori's body is the rolling pin — a falling, spinning axle — and uke is the dough that can't let go, wrapped around the rotation and carried into the ground.

image prompt

A wooden rolling pin tumbling off the edge of a kitchen counter in mid-air, with a large sheet of pale dough wrapped tightly around its barrel, the loose end of the dough trailing up toward the countertop where it was rolled. Flour dust in the air, floor visible below. Side view composition emphasizing the rotation of the falling pin and the dough spiraling around it.

4.4

Sukui Nage 掬投

Scooping Throw

Tori dives their arms below uke's hips or thighs and scoops upward, lifting uke's base clean off the floor so the top-heavy body tips over backward with nothing left under it.

Snow shovel under the snowman

A kid drives a big flat snow shovel under the base of a snowman and heaves up on the handle. The whole snowman leaves the ground on the shovel blade and tips over backward, top ball first, into the snowbank.

The shovel blade under the base is tori's scooping arms under the hips — lift the foundation and the tall heavy body above it has no choice but to topple.

A child in winter clothes levering a wide flat snow shovel under the bottom ball of a full three-ball snowman, the snowman lifted slightly off the ground on the shovel blade and tipping backward at a steep angle, its stick arms flailing and carrot nose pointing at the sky, about to land in a deep snowbank behind it. Side-on composition in a snowy yard.

4.5

Utsuri Goshi 移腰

Shifting Hip

Uke attacks and tori answers by hoisting them into the air with a rear bear-hug arch — then, while uke is still floating, tori swaps their own hips underneath and folds uke over the newly arrived hip to finish the throw.

Bounced over the backyard fence

A kid bounces way too high off a trampoline and drifts over the yard's rail fence. At the top of the float the rail arrives under their belly, they jackknife over it, and flip down into the neighbor's flowerbed.

The trampoline is tori's initial lift that floats uke, and the fence rail is the hip slid in mid-air — a new pivot appearing under a floating body that folds and rotates over it.

image prompt

A backyard scene with a round trampoline in the foreground and a waist-high wooden rail fence beside it. A child is caught in mid-air above the fence, body folded at the waist over the top rail, legs kicking up behind, having floated off the trampoline, about to somersault down into a flowerbed on the far side. Side-on composition showing the arc from trampoline to fence.

4.6

O Guruma 大車

Major Wheel

Tori pulls uke forward onto a straight leg laid high across the front of uke's thighs, and uke's moving body wheels around that leg like a spoke around an axle, rotating fully over before landing.

Full speed into the turnstile

Someone sprints for the train and hits a locked waist-high turnstile bar at full speed. Their hips stop dead on the bar, their legs whip up behind them, and they pinwheel clean over the top.

The fixed bar across the hips is tori's outstretched leg — momentum above the bar keeps going, the body rotates around it as an axle, and the wheel completes.

A subway station entrance where a running commuter has hit a locked waist-high steel turnstile bar and is cartwheeling over it: hips pinned on the bar, torso pitched down on the far side, legs swinging high in the air behind, a shoulder bag flying loose. Side-on composition with the turnstile as the clear pivot point at the center of the rotation.

4.7

Soto Makikomi 外巻込

Outer Wraparound

Tori traps uke's arm tight against their own body, turns in from the outside, and throws themself into a falling spin — uke, tethered by the trapped arm, is whipped around tori's rotating body and driven into the mat.

Tetherball pole tipping mid-whip

The tetherball rope has wound almost all the way in, the ball whipping in tighter, faster circles — and then the whole pole tips over. The ball, wrapped fast to the pole, gets slung around and down with it, thumping into the dirt still tied on.

Uke's trapped arm is the rope, tori's spinning, falling body is the winding pole — wrapped tight to a rotating axle that itself goes to the ground, you go with it.

image prompt

A playground tetherball pole caught in the act of tipping over, the yellow ball on a taut rope wound several turns around the pole, ball whipping around low and fast toward the ground as the pole leans past its balance point, dust kicking up where the pole's base is tearing loose. Dynamic side-on composition showing the spiral of rope around the falling pole.

4.8

Uki Otoshi 浮落

Floating Drop

Tori never lifts or hooks anything — as uke steps forward, tori drops to one knee and steers with the hands alone, wheeling uke's committed momentum around a corner that leads straight into the floor.

The bull and the vanishing cape

The bull charges the cape with everything it has, and the matador simply sweeps the cape down and aside. The bull's own speed carries it past the empty space, nose over hooves, tumbling into the sand.

Tori's hands are the cape — nothing pushes the bull over; its own committed charge is redirected downward around a pivot that isn't there anymore.

Dai Gokyo 第五教

5.1

Osoto Guruma 大外車

Major Outer Wheel

Tori drives uke's upper body backward while stepping in deep, laying one leg as a rigid bar across the back of BOTH of uke's legs. With both legs blocked at once, uke can't step back to save themselves and wheels over the bar onto their back.

Backing over the garden fence

Someone strolls backward without looking and a knee-high garden fence catches them across the back of both legs at the same instant. Their feet stop dead, their shoulders keep going, and they rotate helplessly over the rail like a plank tipping over a ledge.

The fence is tori's extended leg: a fixed horizontal bar behind both knees that turns a backward push into a wheel, because neither foot can step back to rescue balance.

5.2

Uki Waza 浮技

Floating Technique

As uke drives forward into tori, tori simply drops to their own side and stretches one leg out as a low block in front of uke's advancing foot. Uke's own forward momentum, plus tori's pull, sends them sailing over it — tori never lifts anything.

Sprinter hits the tripwire

A kid is running flat-out across the yard when their shin catches a garden hose pulled taut just above the grass. The hose barely moves; the kid leaves the ground entirely, arms out, flying in a long flat arc while the person holding the hose has dropped to the ground to keep it low and tight.

Uke supplies all the energy: forward momentum meeting a low anchored block at the ankle converts a run into flight, just as tori's sacrificed, dropped body anchors the leg-block that launches uke.

5.3

Yoko Wakare 横分

Side Separation

Uke leans their weight into tori, and tori vanishes: dropping flat onto their side with both legs stretched across in front of uke's feet, while their grip yanks uke's upper body forward and down. Uke's support disappears and they pitch headlong over tori's lying body.

The folding table collapses

Someone plants both hands on a folding table and leans their whole weight onto it. The legs snap shut, the tabletop drops flat to the floor — and it's now a low ledge right in front of their feet. With nothing holding them up and their hands still going down with the table, they pitch straight over it.

The collapsing table is tori's sacrifice: the support uke leans on suddenly drops flat, becoming the very obstacle at their feet that their falling weight carries them over.

5.4

Yoko Guruma 横車

Side Wheel

When uke is bent forward over tori, tori throws a leg through, drops onto their own side, and uke's forward momentum carries them wheeling in a big circular arc over tori's fallen body, landing on their back beyond it. Tori's body on the ground is the axle of the wheel.

Rolling over the fallen barrel

Stumbling forward, you run into a big barrel lying on its side right at your knees. It rolls as you hit it, your hips ride up over the curve, and you wheel through one full arc over the top — landing flat on your back on the far side.

The lying barrel is tori's dropped body: a round axle on the ground that catches a forward-committed mass and turns its momentum into one big circular throw over the pivot.

5.5

Ushiro Goshi 後腰

Rear Hip

As uke turns in for a hip throw, tori sinks low behind them, hugs their waist, and heaves them straight up off the floor on tori's hips — then tilts and pulls the platform away so uke drops flat onto their back.

Bucked off the bull

A rodeo bull heaves its back skyward and the rider pops clean off the saddle, weightless for a heartbeat with nothing under them. The bull is already somewhere else; all that's left is the drop, flat onto their back in the dirt.

The bull is tori's hips: a living platform that hurls the rider vertically and then moves out from underneath, so the fall is pure gravity onto the flat of the back.

5.6

Ura Nage 裏投

Rear Throw

Tori clamps uke's torso in a bear hug, drives their own hips under, and arches backward like a drawn bow — hurling uke up and over tori's shoulder to land behind him. Tori sacrifices his own back to turn his whole arching body into a launcher.

The catapult lets fly

A catapult's arm whips up and back, and the pumpkin in its cradle is slung high overhead, sailing in a steep arc to smash down behind the machine. The frame slams itself backward to its stop — the payload keeps going.

Tori's arching body is the catapult arm rotating about its base: the clamped payload is accelerated up and over the top of the arc and released to fall behind the thrower.

5.7

Sumi Otoshi 隅落

Corner Drop

The famous 'air throw' — no leg contact at all. The instant uke's weight settles onto a rear heel, tori drives their hands down toward that unsupported back corner, pushing uke's balance past the edge of their base. Gravity does the rest.

Tipping the chair too far

A kid rocks their chair back onto its two rear legs, then one inch too far. There's a frozen instant where the chair is past the balance point — nothing is touching them, nothing pushed hard, but nothing on earth can bring them back. Over they go, backward into the corner.

The chair's rear legs are uke's rear heel: once the center of gravity drifts past that corner of the base, the fall is inevitable without any sweep, hook, or lift — balance alone was the weapon.

5.8

Yoko Gake 横掛

Side Prop

Tori sweeps the side of uke's ankle with the sole of the foot while throwing their own body to the mat, dragging uke sideways. Uke's feet whip out from under them laterally and they land flat on their side.

The rug yanked flat out

The rug is tori's sweeping foot removing uke's support sideways at floor level, and the puller falling backward with the rug is tori's sacrifice — total commitment of body weight to whip the feet away.
