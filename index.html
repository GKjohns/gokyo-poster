Gokyo Analogies

All 40 throws of the traditional Gokyo no Waza, each paired with the everyday scene that carries its mechanics. Review pass — images and styling come later.

Dai Ikkyo 第一教

1.1

Deashi Harai 出足払

Advancing Foot Sweep

Just as uke's advancing foot is about to take weight, tori sweeps it sideways with the sole of his foot while his hands pull down and across. The support disappears at the exact moment it's needed, and uke's body keeps traveling with nothing under it.

Stepping onto a runaway skateboard

You stride confidently forward and your front foot lands on a skateboard you didn't see. The instant your weight commits, the board scoots your foot out ahead of you, and the rest of you drops flat where the foot used to be.

The advancing foot is carried away sideways at the precise moment of weight transfer, exactly like the timed sweep of deashi harai.

image prompt

A person mid-stride on a sidewalk whose front foot has just landed on a skateboard that is shooting out ahead of them; the foot is being carried away on the board while their body, arms windmilling, hangs in the air beginning to fall flat; the gap between the sliding foot and the falling body is the focus of the composition.

1.2

Hiza Guruma 膝車

Knee Wheel

Tori places the sole of his foot against the side of uke's knee, fixing it in place, while his hands pull uke's upper body around and over. The stopped knee becomes the axle and uke's whole body wheels over it.

Over the handlebars

A kid on a bike rolls into a low curb. The front wheel stops dead, but the rider doesn't: the bike pivots over the jammed wheel and the kid sails clean over the handlebars.

A low fixed block (the knee, the stopped wheel) becomes a pivot while momentum above it keeps going, so the body rotates over the block like a wheel around its axle.

image prompt

A child on a bicycle whose front wheel has just slammed into a low concrete curb; the bike is tipping forward, rear wheel lifting off the ground, and the helmeted rider is mid-air arcing over the handlebars, body rotating around the stopped front wheel; side view showing the curb, the pivoting bike, and the flying rider in one arc.

1.3

Sasae Tsurikomi Ashi 支釣込足

Propping Drawing Ankle

Tori props the front of uke's ankle with his foot, stopping the lower body cold, while his hands lift and pull the upper body forward. Feet stop, torso keeps going, and uke flips over the propped ankle.

The classic classroom trip

A kid hurries down the aisle between desks and another kid slides a sneaker out right in front of their ankle. The foot stops mid-stride; the rest of them doesn't. They pitch straight over the outstretched shoe, books sailing.

The outstretched foot propping the ankle is exactly tori's blocking foot: the base is arrested at floor level while momentum carries the upper body up and over it.

image prompt

A school classroom aisle where one seated kid has slid a sneakered foot out in front of a walking kid's ankle; the walker's foot is stopped dead against the shoe while their body pitches forward into a flat mid-air flip, books and papers flying from their arms; side view at desk height showing the propped ankle as the pivot.

1.4

Uki Goshi 浮腰

Floating Hip

Tori slips his hip in front of uke's and, with an arm around the waist, twists. Uke doesn't get lifted so much as whirled around the hip, which acts as a post his body wraps around on the way to the floor.

Slung by the revolving door

You push into a revolving door in a hurry and lean into the bar. The door takes your push, spins on its axis, and whips you around with it, slinging you sideways out of the doorway before you can get a foot under you.

The door's central axis at hip height converts your straight-ahead push into a fast rotation around it, just as tori's inserted hip turns uke's forward pressure into a wrapping spin to the floor.

image prompt

A glass revolving door mid-spin with a person being whipped around inside it, hips pressed against the push bar, feet swinging off the ground as the rotation slings them sideways out of the door's opening; view from just outside the doorway catching the body mid-whirl around the central axis.

1.5

Osoto Gari 大外刈

Major Outer Reap

Tori rocks uke's weight back onto one heel, then swings his own leg through like a scythe and reaps that supporting leg out from behind. With the weight already behind the base and the base suddenly gone, uke falls straight back.

The sled leaves without you

You plant one boot on a sled parked at the top of a hill and lean back to shout at your friends. The sled sets off downhill behind you and takes that boot with it. Your foot goes one way, backward and up, and the rest of you goes down flat in the snow.

Your weight is already committed backward over one foot, and then that foot is carried away backward under you, which is exactly the direction osoto gari reaps the supporting leg.

image prompt

Side view of a snowy hillside, one frozen instant, whimsical cartoon drawing: a laughing child faces uphill and leans far BACKWARD, one boot planted flat on a wooden sled behind them and the other foot already lifted clear of the snow; the sled has started sliding DOWNHILL and BACKWARD away behind the child, carrying that planted boot with it so the leg is dragged backward and upward and is now higher than the child's hips; the child's shoulders and head are dropping BACKWARD and DOWN in that same downhill direction, back about to slap flat onto the snow, arms windmilling, scarf and knitted hat flying; the boot never leaves the sled, and sled and falling body travel the same way, backward down the slope; loose powder sprays backward along the sled's path; comic cartoon peril, delighted face, nobody hurt; low side-on view so the escaping sled and the flat backward fall read in one line. The sled, the boot planted on it, and the child's falling head and shoulders all travel in the SAME direction, downhill; the child's back lands on the downhill side toward the sled, never uphill away from it, and there is no forward slip in the opposite direction.

1.6

O Goshi 大腰

Major Hip Throw

Tori drops his hips below uke's center, wraps an arm around the waist, lifts uke onto his hips, and tips forward. Uke's body rolls over the hip like a load over an edge and lands in front.

Dumped from a wheelbarrow

A gardener wheels a friend along in a wheelbarrow, then lifts the handles high. The passenger pivots over the front lip and rolls out onto the grass in a heap while the barrow tips over its front wheel.

Lifting from behind rotates the whole load over a low fixed edge at hip height, exactly how tori's raised hips become the tipping point uke rolls over.

image prompt

Side view of a garden lawn, one frozen instant, whimsical cartoon drawing: a grinning gardener tips a wheelbarrow forward by lifting both handles high, the barrow pivoted steeply over its single front wheel; the laughing passenger inside is rolling out over the FRONT LIP of the barrow as one whole, untwisted body with head, chest and hips all facing the SAME direction and the spine curved in a single smooth forward roll: head down first toward the grass beyond the lip, chest following, hips passing over the lip last, both legs together trailing up behind in the same rotation, both arms reaching forward toward the lawn; correct anatomy throughout, one continuous body, no twist at the waist, no limb facing the wrong way, the passenger clearly separate from the barrow; the front lip of the wheelbarrow is the fixed edge the whole body rotates over; comic cartoon peril, both faces delighted, nobody hurt; side-on composition at barrow height.

1.7

Ouchi Gari 大内刈

Major Inner Reap

Tori steps in, hooks his leg around the inside of uke's leg, and reaps it out backward in the direction of uke's toes while driving the chest straight back. One support scooped from between the legs, uke sits down hard onto his back.

Hooked by the giant crook

A performer stands mid-bow when a giant shepherd's crook slides between their ankles, hooks one leg, and yanks it forward while a stagehand's palm nudges their chest. The leg shoots out and they plop straight down onto their back.

A hook pulls one support out from the inside while a push sends the body backward over the space where the leg used to be, the same two opposing forces as ouchi gari.

image prompt

A person on a stage with one leg being hooked and pulled forward at the ankle by a giant shepherd's crook reaching between their feet, while a hand pushes flat against their chest; they are tipping straight backward, arms flailing, one leg dragged up and forward by the hook.

1.8

Seoi Nage 背負投

Shoulder Throw

Tori turns his back into uke, pulls him tight so uke's weight loads onto his back, then bends and pitches him over the shoulder. Uke's own forward pressure gets redirected up, over, and down in front.

Piggyback ride gone wrong

A kid rides piggyback, arms wrapped over the carrier's shoulders. Then the carrier suddenly bends sharply forward, and the passenger keeps going, sailing over the shoulders and flipping onto the grass in front.

A load held tight against the back is rotated over the shoulder fulcrum by the sudden forward bend, exactly how uke, pulled onto tori's back, travels over the shoulder in seoi nage.

image prompt

Side view of a backyard lawn, one frozen instant, whimsical cartoon drawing: a piggyback carrier has bent sharply FORWARD at the waist and the child who was riding is only JUST PAST the carrier's shoulder, very close and still within arm's reach, the child's hip level with the carrier's ear and one of the child's hands still brushing the carrier's shoulder; the child is tipping head-down in a short, tight arc that will put them on the grass immediately in FRONT of the carrier's feet, a single step away, not sailing across the yard; the carrier's shoulder is the pivot and the whole arc is small and close, with almost no gap between the two figures; both faces laughing; comic cartoon peril, nobody hurt; side-on composition emphasizing how near the child is to the carrier's shoulder. The child goes over the TOP of the carrier's shoulder and comes down in FRONT of the carrier's face, on the side the carrier is facing; the child never slides off the carrier's back and is never behind the carrier.

Dai Nikyo 第二教

2.1

Kosoto Gari 小外刈

Minor Outer Reap

Tori clips uke's heel from the outside just as uke commits weight to it, pushing uke's upper body backward at the same moment. The support foot shoots forward and uke sits down hard onto their back.

Slipping on the banana peel

The cartoon classic: your heel lands on a banana peel and shoots forward ahead of you. Your foot goes where it was headed, just far too fast, and the rest of you drops straight back onto the ground.

The peel carries the weight-bearing heel forward out from under you exactly as the reap does, leaving nothing under your center and dropping you flat on your back.

image prompt

A person mid-slip on a sidewalk, one heel skating forward on a yellow banana peel so that leg shoots out ahead while the body tips straight backward, arms windmilling, groceries flying from a dropped bag; low side-on view so the escaping foot and the backward-falling body read in one line.

2.2

Kouchi Gari 小内刈

Minor Inner Reap

As uke steps forward, tori hooks the advancing heel from the inside and slides it further along its own path while pushing uke straight back. The foot keeps traveling, the hips never arrive, and uke sits down between their own feet.

The just-mopped floor slip

You stride across a gleaming just-mopped supermarket aisle past the yellow warning sign. Your front foot hits the wet tile and keeps on going, sliding out far ahead of you while your body sits straight down behind it.

The slip extends the stepping foot along the exact direction it was already traveling, the same 'help your step keep going' action as the inner reap, leaving the hips unsupported and falling backward.

image prompt

A shopper in a supermarket aisle mid-slip on a shiny wet floor, front foot skating far forward ahead of the body with a spray of water, rear leg buckling, body dropping into a seated backward fall, a yellow wet-floor caution cone standing nearby, viewed from the side.

2.3

Koshi Guruma 腰車

Hip Wheel

Tori wraps an arm around uke's head and neck, plants the hips deep across uke's waist, and pulls the head around. Uke's whole body wheels around the hip like a rim around an axle and lands flat on the far side.

Flipping over the playground bar

A kid folds over a low playground high-bar at the waist, and a friend gives the shoulders a pull. The bar stays put while the whole body wheels around it in one smooth circle, feet chasing head all the way over.

The fixed bar at the waist is the hip serving as an axle, and pulling the head/shoulders around it is exactly the wheel-spoke rotation that carries uke over.

image prompt

A child rotating fully around a waist-high horizontal playground bar, body captured mid-revolution with head leading downward on the far side and legs swinging up over the top, hands of a second child guiding the shoulders around, side view composed so the bar reads as the fixed axle at the center of a circular motion path.

2.4

Tsurikomi Goshi 釣込腰

Lifting-Pulling Hip

Tori's high lapel grip lifts and pulls uke up and forward, like hoisting on a line, while tori drops the hips low across uke's thighs. Drawn up onto that low fulcrum, uke pours over it head-first.

Hauled out of the ball pit

A grown-up grabs both your hands and hauls you up and toward them to get you out of the ball pit. It goes fine until your thighs meet the padded wall. From there you do not step over it, you pour over it, head first, out onto the mat at their feet.

The two-handed haul up and toward the puller is the tsurikomi lifting pull, and the padded rim arriving across your thighs is tori's dropped hip: drawn up onto a low edge, you tip straight over it head first.

image prompt

Side view of an indoor playground, one frozen instant, whimsical cartoon drawing: a grinning adult stands outside a ball pit gripping both hands of a laughing child and hauling them UP and TOWARD the adult's own chest, the two pairs of arms forming one taut line rising up and inward; the child has been drawn up out of the balls until their thighs press against the low padded wall of the pit, and at that contact the child is folding forward over the wall, hips stopped on the padded rim while head and shoulders drop DOWN the outside of the wall toward the mat at the adult's feet and both legs rise UP behind them on the inside of the pit; plastic balls spill over the rim in the same direction the child is going; the padded rim across the thighs is the low pivot the whole body pours over, and the order reads as up first, then over the rim, then head-first down on the adult's side of the wall; comic cartoon peril, everyone laughing, nobody hurt; side-on composition at rim height.

2.5

Okuri Ashi Harai 送足払

Sliding Foot Sweep

As uke moves sideways and their feet come together, tori sweeps both feet along in the direction they were already traveling. Both legs sail away together, the body floats flat for an instant, then drops.

Wave sweeps your feet seaward

You stand ankle-deep in the surf as a strong wave rushes sideways along the beach. It scoops both feet and carries them along together, and for one weightless moment you're lying flat on the air before you splash down.

The wave takes both feet at once in the direction the body was already drifting: the same 'send the feet on ahead' action, floating the body horizontal before it falls.

image prompt

A person ankle-deep at the edge of the ocean caught at the instant a rushing sideways wave sweeps both of their feet together off the sand, legs carried sideways by the white water, body momentarily horizontal in the air above the foam, beach and shoreline receding behind, low side-on viewpoint emphasizing the swept feet and floating body.

2.6

Tai Otoshi 体落

Body Drop

Tori blocks uke's shin with an outstretched leg, a low bar across the ankle, and hurls uke's upper body forward and down past it. The feet stop dead at the block while the body keeps going, so uke pitches straight over.

Dog bolts at the low bench

You're walking a big dog past a knee-high park bench when it bolts after a squirrel. The leash snaps taut and hauls you forward and down just as your shins catch the bench edge: feet stopped, everything else launched.

The lunging leash is tori's two-handed hurl on sleeve and lapel, and the bench edge is the outstretched leg: a hard forward-down pull above a shin-height block pitches the body straight over.

image prompt

A person walking a large happy dog past a knee-high park bench at the exact moment the dog bolts after a squirrel: the leash is stretched taut and hauling them forward and down, both shins caught against the bench edge, body pitching comically headlong over it with arms outstretched, the squirrel dashing away at the edge of the frame; side view showing the taut leash line and the bench as the low pivot.

2.7

Harai Goshi 払腰

Sweeping Hip

Tori draws uke's chest forward onto the hip, then sweeps a leg back and up along uke's thighs. The legs are swept skyward while the upper body is already committed over the pivot, so uke rotates over and lands beyond it.

Leapfrog partner stands up

You launch into a leapfrog vault with both hands on your friend's crouched back. Halfway over, your friend decides to stand up. Their back lifts your middle, their shoulders take your thighs skyward, and instead of clearing them you wheel right over the top and land on the far side.

The crouched back is tori's hip, a fixed pivot at your waist, and the sudden rise is the sweeping leg lifting your thighs from behind, so a committed upper body rotates all the way over.

image prompt

Side view of a grassy schoolyard, one frozen instant, whimsical cartoon drawing: a laughing child is folded over the back of a second crouching child in a leapfrog vault, the vaulter's beltline resting square on top of the crouching child's back so that back is the pivot at hip height; the crouching child is grinning and RISING, knees straightening and their back driving UPWARD, which throws the vaulter's thighs UP and BACKWARD toward the sky so both of the vaulter's legs are already above head height with heels pointing at the clouds; at the same time the vaulter's head and shoulders are committed DOWNWARD on the far side of the crouching child, hands reaching for the grass beyond and hair hanging straight down; the vaulter is frozen at the top of one continuous forward rotation around the crouching back as an axle, legs up on the near side and head down on the far side; a cap and an apple tumble loose; both faces delighted, comic cartoon peril, nobody hurt; side-on composition at hip height so the crouching back reads clearly as the fulcrum of the rotation.

2.8

Uchi Mata 内股

Inner Thigh Throw

Tori's leg rises powerfully between uke's thighs, lifting one inner thigh skyward while the hands pull uke's upper body forward and around. Lifted at the middle and pulled at the top, uke rotates over the rising leg.

Stepping over the dog who stands up

You are stepping over the big dog asleep across the hallway, front foot already down on the far side, back leg still passing over him. He picks that moment to stand up, back end first. His rump rises under your inner thigh, that leg goes straight up toward the ceiling, and since you were already leaning into the step you go head-first over his ears.

The dog's rising back is tori's sweeping leg, lifting from the inside of one thigh, and your forward lean into the step is the hand-pull. Lift below, lean above, one leg skyward and the head goes over: that is uchi mata.

image prompt

Side view of a hallway rug, one frozen instant, whimsical cartoon drawing: a big shaggy dog had been lying flat across the hallway and is now springing to its feet HINDQUARTERS FIRST, back legs already straight and rump high, front legs still folded and head still low near the floor, so its back slopes steeply DOWN from tail to nose; a laughing child was mid-step over the dog, front foot already planted on the rug on the far side of the dog and trailing leg still passing over the dog's back; the dog's rising rump has come up under the INNER THIGH of that trailing leg and is lifting the whole leg high into the air behind the child, foot pointing to the ceiling, the thigh resting across the top of the dog's rump like a rider's leg; the child's upper body, already leaning forward into the step, is pitching FORWARD and DOWN over the dog's lowered head, both hands reaching for the rug ahead, head lower than hips; the child's other foot stays planted on the rug beside the dog's shoulder as the only support; the child sits across the dog's back at the thigh, no part of the dog is at the child's middle; dog tail wagging, child laughing; comic cartoon surprise, nobody hurt; low side-on composition at dog height so the rising rump under one thigh and the forward pitch read as one motion.

Dai Sankyo 第三教

3.1

Kosoto Gake 小外掛

Minor Outer Hook

Tori hooks a foot behind uke's heel from the outside and pins it to the mat, then drives their weight straight into uke's chest. With the foot anchored and unable to step back, uke topples backward over their own trapped leg.

Boot stuck in the mud

One boot is sunk to the ankle in deep mud when a friend gives you a playful shove on the chest. The rescue step backward never comes, the boot won't budge, so you tip straight over it like a fence post, flat onto your back.

The mud is tori's hooking foot pinning the heel to the ground: with the support anchored, the chest-high push has nothing to do but rotate you straight over backward.

image prompt

A muddy field where one person's boot is sunk deep in the mud, leg anchored, while a friend's outstretched hand has just shoved their chest; the stuck person is tipping straight backward like a felled post, the anchored leg still upright in the mud, arms flailing. Side view emphasizing the trapped boot as the fixed point of the fall.

3.2

Tsuri Goshi 釣腰

Lifting Hip

Tori reaches over or under uke's arm and grabs the belt like a handle, hauls uke up onto the hip with that grip ('tsuri' means fishing) and rotates them over the hip to the mat.

Hauling the big fish over the rail

A fisherman has hooked a huge fish and heaves it up out of the water on a taut line. At the top of the pull the fish swings up and over the boat's rail, pivoting on the gunwale and flopping down onto the deck on the other side.

The fishing line is the belt grip (a single handle doing all the lifting) and the boat rail is tori's hip: hoist the load up on the handle, let it pivot over the rail, and it lands on the far side.

image prompt

A small fishing boat on calm water: a fisherman leans back hauling a taut fishing line with both hands, and an enormous fish is caught mid-air at the top of the arc, swinging up and over the boat's side rail, about to flop onto the deck. The fish's body curves over the rail like a load pivoting on a fulcrum. Side view showing the line, the rail contact point, and the fish's arc.

3.3

Yoko Otoshi 横落

Side Drop

A sacrifice throw: tori deliberately falls onto their own side, sliding an extended leg against the outside of uke's feet. Tori's falling body weight, transmitted through the grips, drags uke sideways over their own blocked feet.

Dragged into the pool

Two friends stand holding wrists at the edge of a pool. One suddenly lets themselves fall in, and the raised pool edge catches the other friend's feet while the falling friend's whole weight yanks them sideways, so they pitch helplessly over the coping and into the water too.

The falling friend is tori's sacrificed body weight doing the pulling, and the raised pool edge is tori's extended leg blocking uke's feet: anchored feet plus a great weight dropping beside you equals a sideways crash.

image prompt

A backyard swimming pool with a raised stone edge: one person is mid-fall into the water, back-first with a splash beginning, still gripping the wrist of a second person on the deck. The second person's feet are caught against the raised pool edge and their body is tipping sideways over it, off balance, about to be pulled in. Side-on composition showing the edge blocking the feet and the falling body's weight dragging through the joined arms.

3.4

Ashi Guruma 足車

Leg Wheel

Tori turns in and holds an extended leg as a rigid horizontal bar across the front of uke's knees, then pulls uke's upper body forward and around. Uke's legs stop at the bar while their body keeps traveling, so they wheel around the leg like a wheel around an axle.

Flipping over the low chain fence

A kid sprints across a park and doesn't see the knee-high chain slung between two posts. The chain stops their shins cold, their upper body keeps going, and they somersault clean over it, feet flying up over their head.

The taut chain is tori's extended leg, a fixed bar at knee height, and the runner's own momentum is tori's pull: body rotates over the bar because the legs are stopped and the torso isn't.

image prompt

Side view of a park path, one frozen instant, whimsical cartoon drawing: two short posts with a chain strung between them at knee height; a running child has just hit that chain with BOTH SHINS and is frozen at the very first instant of tripping: both shins are pressed hard against the chain just below the knees, the chain visibly BOWING forward under the shins and plainly touching them, and this shin-to-chain contact is the center of the picture; the child's sneakers are just leaving the ground BEHIND the chain, toes trailing, heels up; above the stopped shins the child's whole body is wheeling FORWARD and DOWN over the chain, hips the highest point, back arched, head already LOWER than the hips and diving toward the grass on the far side, both arms stretched out ahead with the hands reaching for the ground beyond the chain; the child is not yet flying above the chain, the shins are still on it and the body is pivoting over that point like a wheel over an axle; the child is one complete, correctly proportioned figure with two arms and two legs all properly attached, entirely separate from the posts, and the chain touches only the shins and never passes through the torso; a scattering of leaves follows the arc; comic cartoon peril, laughing face, nobody hurt; low side-on composition at chain height.

3.5

Hane Goshi 跳腰

Spring Hip

Tori tucks in close with one bent, springy leg pressed across uke's thighs and the hip loaded under uke. Then the leg snaps straight with a hop ('hane' means spring), bouncing uke up off the coiled leg and over.

Bucked off the spring rider

You bounce hard on the playground's spring horse and squash the fat coil underneath you flat. The coil is not interested in staying squashed. It snaps straight, pops you off the seat, and sends you over the handle in a slow forward roll.

The coil under the seat is tori's bent, loaded leg, and its release throws you upward off it while the handle in front becomes the low edge you rotate over.

image prompt

Side view of a playground, one frozen instant, whimsical cartoon drawing: a playground spring rider, a small wooden horse mounted on one thick steel coil spring bolted to the ground, caught at the exact instant the coil that had been squashed nearly flat snaps STRAIGHT and UPWARD; the laughing child who was riding it has been launched UP and FORWARD off the seat and floats a hand's height above the saddle, already tipping head-first over the horse's front handle so that both legs swing UP behind them while head and shoulders go DOWN and forward on the far side of the handle; the bolted base of the coil is the fixed pivot of the spring and the handle is the low edge the child rotates over; sand kicks up beneath and a cap flies off; comic cartoon peril, grinning face, nobody hurt; side-on composition showing the straightening coil below and the forward roll above as one motion.

3.6

Harai Tsurikomi Ashi 払釣込足

Sweeping Drawing Ankle

Tori lifts and draws uke upward-forward with both grips, holding uke's weight almost suspended, while sweeping uke's advancing foot sideways before it can land and take weight. The feet fly out from under a body that's being held up from above.

First time on the ice

A friend skates backward holding both your hands, towing you along so half your weight is on their grip. Your blades barely touch the ice, and then they slide out sideways all at once. You end up hanging off two hands with your feet somewhere off to the left.

Being towed by the hands lifts the load off your feet, exactly like the lifting pull, so a small sideways slide sends both feet away before either can take weight.

image prompt

Side view of an ice rink, one frozen instant, whimsical cartoon drawing: a grinning skater glides backward holding both hands of a wobbly beginner, arms stretched and pulling the beginner UP and FORWARD so the beginner's weight plainly hangs from the joined hands, shoulders hoisted and body leaning; the beginner's two skates are still LACED FIRMLY ON THEIR FEET, blades touching the ice, and both feet-with-skates have slid SIDEWAYS out from under them together as one unit, gliding away horizontally to one side across the ice with shaved ice spraying in that same sideways direction, the legs trailing after the skates so both feet are now far outside the line of the body and nothing is under the hips; no skate ever leaves a foot, there are no bare feet and no loose skates anywhere in the picture; the joined hands out in front and above are the only support and the pivot the beginner's body swings from, a long scarf streaming; small skaters in the distance; comic cartoon peril, both faces laughing, nobody hurt; side-on composition showing the upward pull at the hands and the sideways escape of the skated feet at the same instant.

3.7

Tomoe Nage 巴投

Circle Throw

As uke pushes forward, tori drops straight onto their own back, plants a foot on uke's belly, and uses uke's charging momentum plus the extending leg to sling them overhead in a full circle ('tomoe' is a swirling comma shape).

The airplane game gone flying

A parent lies back on the grass playing airplane: foot on the kid's tummy, hands in hands. But this time the leg extends with a whoosh and the kid actually launches, soaring upside down clear over the parent's head, a full loop in the air.

It is the same machine: the parent's planted foot is tori's launching leg, the held hands are the grips steering the circle, and the kid's forward lean is uke's momentum being redirected overhead.

image prompt

A sunny backyard lawn: a parent lies flat on their back with one leg extended straight up, foot planted on a child's belly, hands gripping the child's hands. The child is airborne and inverted, mid-arc directly overhead, hair and shirt tumbling, following a circular flight path over the parent's head. Side view capturing the full circle of the trajectory.

3.8

Kata Guruma 肩車

Shoulder Wheel

Tori drops low, pulls uke's arm so their body folds across tori's shoulders, draped like a load in a fireman's carry, then stands and wheels uke across the shoulders, rolling them off the far side to the mat.

Farmer flips the grain sack

A farmer squats, hoists a huge sack of grain across the shoulders, and carries it balanced behind the neck. At the wagon, one heave, and the sack rolls end over end across the shoulders and tumbles off the far side.

The shoulders are the axle in both: a long load draped across them is lifted from one side and wheeled over to drop off the other, which is exactly what 'shoulder wheel' means.

image prompt

Side view of a farmyard beside a wooden wagon, one frozen instant, whimsical cartoon drawing: a grinning farmer rises out of a squat with an enormous grain sack lying HORIZONTALLY ACROSS THE BACK OF BOTH SHOULDERS, the middle of the sack resting on the nape of the neck so the shoulders carry the whole load; the sack is caught at the instant of the heave, rolling off the FAR shoulder: its far end has already dropped DOWN beyond that shoulder toward the wagon bed while its near end rises UP behind the other shoulder, the whole sack turning end over end about the line of the shoulders; the farmer's shoulders are the visible pivot at the center of that rotation and both of the farmer's hands are still on the sack; grain spills out in an arc following the sack's roll down the far side; comic cheerful farm scene, delighted face, nobody hurt; side-on composition at shoulder height so the shoulders read clearly as the axle and the sack as the load wheeling over them.

Dai Yonkyo 第四教

4.1

Sumi Gaeshi 隅返

Corner Reversal

Tori drops onto their back beneath uke, hooks an instep into uke's inner thigh, and uses the backward roll to loft uke's hips up and over, flipping them past the corner of their own balance.

The airplane game gone too far

A parent lies on their back playing 'airplane,' feet planted in the kid's hips, and rocks backward with just a little too much enthusiasm. The kid sails clean over the parent's head, arms out, headed for the couch cushions behind.
